Cookie Policy

This website uses cookies. Below you can read which cookies we place, for what purpose, and how you can change your choice.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make a website function properly and to collect information about your use.

2. Which cookies do we use?

TypePurposeConsent required?
Necessary cookiesRemembering your cookie choice and ensuring the website functions correctlyNo
Google MapsDisplaying the interactive map with our location. Google may process data in doing soYes
Google AdsMeasuring the effectiveness of our advertising (conversion measurement)Yes

3. Consent

On your first visit, we ask via a cookie notice whether you accept non-necessary cookies (Google Maps and Google Ads). We always place necessary cookies, as the website does not function properly without them. We only place the other cookies after you have given your consent. For Google Ads we use Google Consent Mode.

4. Changing or withdrawing your choice

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by deleting the cookies of this website in your browser. On your next visit, the cookie notice will appear again and you can make a new choice. You can also manage or delete cookies via your browser settings.

5. More information

You can read more about how we handle personal data in our privacy policy. If you have any questions, contact us at info@becenter.nl or +31 (0)6 2547 2921.
