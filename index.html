Location

Central in Limburg, easy to reach

BeCenter is located on the town square of Swalmen, in the municipality of Roermond. The central location in Mid-Limburg offers excellent accessibility by car and public transport, ideal for entrepreneurs doing business throughout the Netherlands and beyond.

Swalmen combines the tranquility of the Limburg countryside with the proximity of urban amenities in Roermond, including the Designer Outlet and Roermond Central Station with direct connections to Eindhoven, Maastricht and the Randstad.

  • Direct access to the A73 motorway (Swalmen exit)
  • Centrally located between Germany and Belgium
  • Free parking on private grounds
  • Bus stop and train station within walking distance
  • Restaurants & amenities right next door
  • Professional reception for national and international entrepreneurs
