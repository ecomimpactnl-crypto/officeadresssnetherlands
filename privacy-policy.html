Privacy Policy

This policy has been drawn up to comply with the GDPR and aims to ensure that data is protected and treated confidentially. Here you can read which data we collect for the services of BeCenter and why.

1. Purpose and scope

1.1 Purpose of the GDPR policy

This policy has been drawn up to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and aims to ensure that data is protected and treated confidentially. In this policy you can read which data we collect for our services at BeCenter (hereinafter: “BeCenter” or “we” or “us/our”) and why we collect this data. We respect the privacy of all users of our services and we ensure that the personal information provided to us is treated confidentially. We act in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We process the following personal data on the basis of the grounds described below.

1.2 Who is BeCenter and to whom does this privacy policy apply?

BeCenter is established and has its office at Markt 19, 6071 JD Swalmen (Netherlands) and is registered in the Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) under number 70467110. You can reach us by phone on +31 (0)6 2547 2921 and by email at info@becenter.nl.

This privacy policy applies to:

  • customers of BeCenter;
  • potential customers with whom BeCenter has made or had contact;
  • employees of BeCenter.

As an organisation, we refer to our privacy policy on our website and in all documents containing personal data (employment agreement, rental agreement, registration form).

2. Collection of information

2.1 Collection of information

When customers register for one of our services, we ask them to provide the following personal information required to process the request:

Personal data

  • First name(s) and surname;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Mobile telephone number;
  • Private address;
  • Postcode and place of residence;
  • Country;
  • Passport number and/or ID card number;
  • Date of birth;
  • Place and country of birth;
  • Nationality;
  • Copy of passport and/or ID card;
  • Proof of residential address no older than 2 months.

Company data

  • Company name and legal form;
  • Registered address;
  • Postal address;
  • Website;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Business activities;
  • Motivation for registration;
  • Number of employees;
  • Position;
  • Chamber of Commerce (KVK) number;
  • Chamber of Commerce (KVK) extract;
  • Deed of incorporation;
  • UBO registration;
  • UBO declaration;
  • PEP declaration.

Other personal data provided by the customer:

  • Contact details and other personal data provided via email.
  • Contact details provided during introductory meetings, such as information stated on business cards.

2.2 Use of social media

We may receive information about customers from other sources, such as social media platforms, in accordance with their privacy policies, and combine this information with the data we already have about the customer.

2.3 Consent of minors

As an organisation, we declare that we only process personal data of persons under the age of 16 if written consent has been given for this by the parent, carer or legal representative.

3. Use and sharing of information

3.1 Use of information

We use the information collected for the performance of the agreement to which the customer is a party, the provision of the requested services, supporting customer needs and communication with customers. We wish to keep our customers informed about the development of our services by means of newsletters. We also use the information to comply with legal obligations based on laws and/or regulations.

3.2 Sharing of information

We only share the personal information of our customers as described in this privacy policy or when necessary for the provision of our services. We may share customer data with legal bodies or authorities if we are required to do so on the basis of laws and/or regulations.

4. Rights

Customers have certain rights regarding their personal information, namely the right to information, access and control, correction, erasure, data portability and objection to processing.

5. Data security

5.1 Data security of digital documents

We strive to safeguard the security of customer data and take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect their personal information against misuse, unauthorised conduct or disclosure. Customer data is stored on our own secured computers and secured servers of a third party (BOX), with which party (the “processor”) a processing agreement has been concluded that meets the requirements set by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

As an organisation, we declare that we never pass on personal data to other parties with whom we have not concluded a processing agreement, where this is necessary for the performance of the purposes for which we obtained them.

As an organisation, we have stored the personal data only on computers with security software, whereby both the security software and the operating system are set to automatically retrieve and install updates.

As an organisation, we have always stored personal data behind the security of at least a username and password and have secured the stored personal data with a backup.

5.2 Data security of physical documents

Paper files and documents containing personal data are stored in a locked room that is only accessed by our responsible employees. The employees of BeCenter are obliged to respect the confidentiality of customer data. The GDPR is discussed annually during the work meeting.

6. Retention period

Personal data of customers is stored carefully and no longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained. Unless BeCenter is required to retain customer data for longer on the basis of legislation, we apply the statutory retention periods.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Legislation and developments move quickly and, as a result, something may sometimes change in the personal data we request from customers and the way in which we use their personal data. Changes will be communicated by email if necessary. This privacy policy was last amended on 12.01.2026.

8. Complaints handling

If customers have a complaint about the use of their personal data, they can contact us. We aim to find a joint solution. If we are nevertheless unable to reach a solution together, they can submit their complaint to the competent court or to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). If they have questions about our privacy policy or the way in which we process their personal information, they can contact us by phone on +31 (0)6 2547 2921 or by email at info@becenter.nl.
